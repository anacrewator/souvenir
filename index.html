      How it works

      1. It reads every photo's label

        Phones save when and where each shot was taken, inside the file. Videos too. Google Takeout's extra files fill the gaps.

      2. It works out where home is

        The place you take photos on the most days is home. Photos far from it, close together in time, become a trip.

      3. You get the albums

        Rename them, flip through each day, save them as folders or a ZIP, or make a story card to post.
